---
title: 'Bots across homeservers'
description: 'How bots work in guilds on other homeservers, and what homeserver admins can configure.'
---

Bots federate like people do. A bot on `alpha.example` can be added to a guild hosted on `beta.example`, and people from
`gamma.example` in that guild can use its commands and buttons. Your code doesn't change: it only ever talks to the
bot's own homeserver.

## How it works

- **Adding** a bot asks the guild's homeserver, which checks that you may add bots there (owner or admin), then asks the
  bot's homeserver whether the bot agrees. Public bots always agree. A private bot only joins when its owner asked for
  it, starting from the bot's own homeserver, which gives the guild's homeserver a one-time permission for that one
  guild.
- **Events** of the guild reach the bot's homeserver like they reach any member's, and from there the bot's socket.
- **Interactions** are checked by the guild's homeserver, which knows the guild, its members, its messages and the
  bot's commands. It sends them to the bot's homeserver, and the bot's answers go back the same way. Private answers go
  straight to the person's own homeserver, which only accepts them for guilds that homeserver actually hosts, and only
  for a while after the person used a command or button there.
- **Trust**: a bot's homeserver controls the bot, so treat a remote bot like any member from that homeserver. It can
  only do what its role allows in your guild, it can't add other bots, and it can always be removed: whether an account
  is a bot is fixed when your homeserver first sees it.
- **Removing** a bot from a guild tells its homeserver, and a bot leaving tells the guild's homeserver.

Both homeservers need a version of Anchor with bots. With an older one, adding a bot fails with a clear message.

## For homeserver admins

Bots need no setup. To limit them, add this to `config.toml`:

```toml
[bots]
# whether people on this homeserver can make bots (existing bots keep working)
enabled = true
# how many bots each person can make
max_per_user = 25
```
